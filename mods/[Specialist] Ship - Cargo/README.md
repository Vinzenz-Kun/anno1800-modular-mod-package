# [Specialist] Ellis Ward, the Cargo Purser

I wanted a long trade route to work with fewer ships. Ellis makes that possible by cutting travel and loading time, and you can assign the purser to just the route that needs help.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Adds 60% sailing speed and 150% loading speed, removes cargo and damage slowdown and sets active purchase prices to 70%. Equip in a ship item slot before shopping at a trader or assigning the ship to a trade route.

## Availability

Look for legendary offers from Eli Bleakworth, Anne Harlow or Jean La Fortune. Trading with a pirate requires trade rights. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Ship - Cargo` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
