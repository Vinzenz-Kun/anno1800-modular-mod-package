# [Gameplay] Affordable, fast research

I wanted room to experiment with different specialists during the same play session. Use this module if long development queues and repeated Research Point costs get in the way of trying a new combination.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

The Research Institute develops items and major discoveries in five to sixty seconds, depending on assigned workforce.

Discovering a recipe costs 50 Research Points. Developing a common, uncommon, rare, epic or legendary item costs 100, 200, 300, 500 or 1,000 points respectively. Major discoveries cost 1,000 and campus extensions cost 100. Repeat developments use the same price. Works with the optional specialist research catalogue. Requires Land of Lions.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Research` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
