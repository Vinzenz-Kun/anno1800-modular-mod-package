# [Gameplay] Depots and final trading posts

I wanted to leave more of the waterfront open for piers and shipyards. With this module, a single depot or fully upgraded trading post can carry much of an island's storage, which makes warehouse expansion a much smaller part of the game.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Each goods depot adds 2,000 tons to island storage. A fully upgraded trading post provides 2,500 tons.

Standard depots in all four regions and Docklands depot modules receive the depot capacity. The post capacity starts at tier four in the Old World and Cape Trelawney, and at tier three in the New World, Enbesa and the Arctic. Both main and imperial posts are covered, including the Arctic plateau's Sky Trading Post. Capacities are totals per building and apply to each storable good. Oil has its own storage system.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Harbour Storage` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
