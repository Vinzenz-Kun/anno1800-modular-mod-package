# [Gameplay] No royal taxes

I wanted players to be able to build a large population centre on one island and keep the income it earns. Choose this module if you enjoy concentrating your city and want to remove the tax incentive to spread residents across several islands.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Royal tax is zero for every population tier in the game’s royal tax table.

After a settlement passes its population threshold, each further tax interval still contributes zero percent royal tax. Check the royal tax line in the finance statistics for a large island.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Royal Taxes` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
