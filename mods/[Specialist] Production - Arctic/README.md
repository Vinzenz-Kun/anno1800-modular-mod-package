# [Specialist] Jack of All Lodges

I made Jack for the moment when a new Arctic settlement needs warm shelters, parkas and sleds all at once. Giving him his own Lodge slot lets you decide which settlement gets through that first winter so easily.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in an Arctic Lodge. Provides heat to compatible buildings. Every completed production cycle adds one ton of each of the 99 goods in the Arctic catalogue. Productivity +100%. Each eligible producer must finish a cycle to generate its extras.

## Bonus goods per cycle

The catalogue includes physical island goods assigned to the region, used by its residents or used in local production and construction. Shared goods and imports can appear in several regions. Jack also supplies materials used in Old Nate's workshop recipes. Mail and reports use the game's postal storage and delivery rules.

The earlier Arctic Lodge specialist also supplied goods from other regions. Jack keeps its extra imports of Advanced Weapons, Aluminium Profiles, Bombins, Bricks, Celluloid, Ceramics, Champagne, Cigars, Clay Pipe, Dried Meat, Ethanol, Finery, Fried Plantains, Fur Coats, Goat Milk, Hibiscus Tea, Illuminated Script, Jewelry, Lacquer, Lanterns, Leather Boots, Light Bulbs, Mud Bricks, Paper, Penny Farthings, Ponchos, Sausages, Seafood Stew, Sewing Machines, Soap, Spectacles, Steam Carriages, Tailored Suits, Tapestries, Tortillas, Weapons, Work Clothes alongside today's regional catalogue.

## Availability

Craft the specialist at Old Nate's Arctic workshop for one Lost Expedition Scrap, or check Qumaq's legendary offers. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Production - Arctic` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.

Playing in the Arctic requires The Passage.
