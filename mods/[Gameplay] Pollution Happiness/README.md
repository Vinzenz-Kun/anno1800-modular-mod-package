# [Gameplay] Pollution and happiness

I wanted the option to build a factory town without planning housing around the happiness penalty from pollution. Use this module to give industrial settlements that advantage while you manage their attractiveness separately.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Residents receive a positive five-point happiness contribution at both ends of the pollution scale.

Open the island happiness breakdown to see the pollution contribution. Factories still affect island attractiveness through their existing pollution values.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Pollution Happiness` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
