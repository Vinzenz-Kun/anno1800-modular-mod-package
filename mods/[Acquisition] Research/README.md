# [Acquisition] Specialist research

I wanted my scholars to be able to find a specialist even when nobody had one for sale. This module adds specialist recipes to the Land of Lions Research Institute. Choose it on its own to keep the usual surprise of a trader's stock.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Discover talents in the production, city and harbour fields. Lodge specialists use production and ship specialists use harbour. Common characters use automatic discovery or the low-tier talent pool. Research costs, institute unlocks and development apply. The regular University is a public service building.

Copy this folder into Anno 1800's mods folder. Install `[Acquisition] Traders` alongside it for both routes. Install this folder alone with selected characters to retain normal trader progression and rerolls. Using the institute requires Land of Lions. Quest cargo, scenario-only characters and narrative rarities have no standard development recipe.
