# [Gameplay] Island-wide tourist venues

I wanted tourists staying at one end of an island to enjoy a venue at the other. Use this module if you prefer choosing a hotel site for the view and are happy to take much of the distance planning out of Tourist Season.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Bus stops, food and drink venues and Iron Tower recipes reach across the island. Connected tourist needs receive their full distance multiplier.

Bus stop activation, road-network range and the recipe effect radius are set to 2,048 units. Connect stops by road, staff each venue and supply the active recipe. Satisfaction can still depend on attractiveness, productivity or other needs. The patch targets Tourist Season venues and their crossover recipes.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Tourism` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
