# [Inventory] Specialist stacks of 50

When I ship the same specialist to several islands, I like to keep the spares in one cargo slot. This module lets up to 50 copies share a stack. You can use it with the game's normal traders and specialists.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Copy this folder into Anno 1800's mods folder. Equipment-slot and duplicate-equipping rules still apply.

## Thanks to Reallizer

Reallizer showed me how much easier a warehouse is to use when identical items can share a stack. I'm grateful for that idea. I kept the familiar limit of fifty and applied it to specialists, including the new characters. Reallizer's original is well worth a visit.
