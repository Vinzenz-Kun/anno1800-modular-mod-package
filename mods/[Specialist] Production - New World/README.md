# [Specialist] Jack of Both Tropics

I wanted to build up a New World island without waiting for every specialist production line to come online. Jack brings the complete New World goods catalogue into each cycle, so you can choose which islands get that head start.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in a Trade Union. Provides electricity to compatible buildings. Every completed production cycle adds one ton of each of the 113 goods in the New World catalogue. Productivity +100%. Each eligible producer must finish a cycle to generate its extras.

## Bonus goods per cycle

The catalogue includes physical island goods assigned to the region, used by its residents or used in local production and construction. Shared goods and imports can appear in several regions. This module lets the trading post store bonus tractor fuel. Tractor barns still receive fuel carts from an operating Fuel Station. Oil still uses the oil harbour's dedicated storage. Mail and reports use the game's postal storage and delivery rules.

## Availability

Look for a legendary specialist at Isabel Sarmento's harbour once legendary offers are unlocked. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Production - New World` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
