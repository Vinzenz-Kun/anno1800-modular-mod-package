# [Rules] Free Fertility

I wanted the freedom to choose a farm island for its coastline and layout, even when its soil was wrong for a favourite crop. This rule makes that possible across the four standard regions. I kept it separate because choosing islands for their fertilities can be half the fun of settling them.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Farms and producers can operate on islands lacking their normal fertility. Physical deposits, fields, pastures, irrigation and other inputs still follow the building rules. This change works independently of Robin Vale and applies to existing buildings.

## Installation

Copy `[Rules] Free Fertility` into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
