# [Specialist] Robin Vale, the Field Naturalist

I designed Robin for islands where one more plantation would swallow the last good piece of farmland. With Robin equipped, a crop or livestock farm can work from its main building alone.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Crop fields and animal pens are optional. +100% productivity, -100% required forest/free area and -50% workforce on compatible buildings. Adds one ton of each of 66 agricultural goods per completed cycle. The farm's main building can operate with zero crop fields or animal pens. The item tooltip shows a -400% module modifier. That leaves room for up to +300% from tractors and other field modifiers while keeping the effective requirement at zero.

Equip one Robin variant per catchment. The field modifiers add together if you overlap both editions. Place the Trade Union item in the Old World, Cape Trelawney, New World or Enbesa, and the Lodge item in the Arctic. Both editions require the Free Fertility module, which removes regional fertility requirements for all supported crops. The woodland bonus also reaches lumberjacks, hunters, charcoal kilns, Wanza woodcutters and orchards.

The catalogue combines the game's regional agricultural lists, the outputs of the covered producers, farm supplies and common farm-processing ingredients. Fuel uses the physical Bright Harvest product. The module adds a warehouse slot for bonus fuel. Keep an operating Fuel Station connected to tractor barns for their local cart deliveries.

## Availability

Look for a legendary specialist at Eli Bleakworth's prison once legendary offers are unlocked. The Lodge edition is also craftable for one Lost Expedition Scrap at Old Nate's Arctic workshop. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Agriculture` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
