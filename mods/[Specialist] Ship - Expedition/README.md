# [Specialist] Dr Rowan Pike, Expedition Polymath

I made Rowan for voyages where I wanted to see the next expedition event without having to assemble a whole crew for its skill checks. You decide which expedition gets that advantage when you choose its crew.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Adds 100 morale, 100 points in all eight expedition disciplines and eleven character perks. Equip or take this specialist along when preparing an expedition. Ship-type checks still depend on the vessel.

## Availability

Look for legendary offers from Eli Bleakworth, Anne Harlow or Jean La Fortune. Trading with a pirate requires trade rights. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Ship - Expedition` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
