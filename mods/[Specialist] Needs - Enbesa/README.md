# [Specialist] Tadesse, Steward of the Highlands

I wanted an Enbesan town to be able to grow even when its supply of everyday goods falls behind. Tadesse puts that choice in the Town Hall, so other settlements can still depend on their farms and workshops.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in a Town Hall. Fulfils unlocked basic, luxury and enabled lifestyle needs, raises happiness by 25 and doubles residence capacity. Covers 26 distinct needs. Provides electricity to compatible buildings. Gives +3 influence to affected buildings with an influence source. Reduces riot, illness and Arctic flu risk by 100% wherever those incidents exist. Public services can also be satisfied by a local staple supplied by the specialist. Tier unlocks and lifestyle activation still apply.

## Availability

The specialist can appear at Emperor Ketema's harbour in Enbesa. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Needs - Enbesa` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.

Playing in Enbesa requires Land of Lions.
