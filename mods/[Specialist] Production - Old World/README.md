# [Specialist] Jack of All Trades

I made Jack an optional shortcut for the point when an Old World city starts asking for steam carriages, telephones and elevators at once. A working factory with Jack can supply the full Old World goods catalogue.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in a Trade Union. Provides electricity to compatible buildings. Every completed production cycle adds one ton of each of the 149 goods in the Old World catalogue. Productivity +100%. Each eligible producer must finish a cycle to generate its extras.

## Bonus goods per cycle

The catalogue includes physical island goods assigned to the region, used by its residents or used in local production and construction. Shared goods and imports can appear in several regions. Jack also supplies materials used in Old Nate's workshop recipes. This module lets the trading post store bonus tractor fuel. Tractor barns still receive fuel carts from an operating Fuel Station. Oil still uses the oil harbour's dedicated storage. Mail and reports use the game's postal storage and delivery rules.

The original Old World specialist also supplied goods from other regions. Jack keeps its extra imports of Ceramics, Dried Meat, Finery, Fried Plantains, Goat Milk, Illuminated Script, Lanterns, Mud Bricks, Ponchos, Tortillas alongside today's regional catalogue.

## Availability

Look for a legendary specialist at Eli Bleakworth's prison once legendary offers are unlocked. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Production - Old World` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
