# [Specialist] Sila, Keeper of the Hearth

I made Sila for Arctic settlements that I wanted to keep alive through a missed delivery. Her Lodge can take over both supplies and heat, which makes surviving the cold a much smaller part of managing that settlement.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in an Arctic Lodge. Fulfils unlocked basic, luxury and enabled lifestyle needs, raises happiness by 25 and doubles residence capacity. Covers 21 distinct needs. Grants heater service to eligible buildings. Arctic heat is included among the residence needs. Gives +3 influence to affected buildings with an influence source. Reduces riot, illness and Arctic flu risk by 100% wherever those incidents exist. Public services can also be satisfied by a local staple supplied by the specialist. Tier unlocks and lifestyle activation still apply.

## Availability

Craft the specialist at Old Nate's Arctic workshop for one Lost Expedition Scrap, or check Qumaq's legendary offers. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Needs - Arctic` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.

Playing in the Arctic requires The Passage.
