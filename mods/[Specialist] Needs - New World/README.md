# [Specialist] Ines Solano, the Community Steward

I wanted the choice to grow a New World neighbourhood without continually adding deliveries as residents move up through the tiers. Give Ines a Town Hall slot wherever you want that relief.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in a Town Hall. Fulfils unlocked basic, luxury and enabled lifestyle needs, raises happiness by 25 and doubles residence capacity. Covers 42 distinct needs. Provides electricity to compatible buildings. Gives +3 influence to affected buildings with an influence source. Reduces riot, illness and Arctic flu risk by 100% wherever those incidents exist. Public services can also be satisfied by a local staple supplied by the specialist. Tier unlocks and lifestyle activation still apply.

## Availability

Look for a legendary specialist at Isabel Sarmento's harbour once legendary offers are unlocked. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Needs - New World` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
