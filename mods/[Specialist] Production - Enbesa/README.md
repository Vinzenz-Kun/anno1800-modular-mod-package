# [Specialist] Jack of All Lions

I wanted Enbesa to have a Jack of its own. With him at work, a single producer can also turn out lanterns, leather boots and illuminated scripts, enough to spare you several supply chains.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in a Trade Union. Provides electricity to compatible buildings. Every completed production cycle adds one ton of each of the 49 goods in the Enbesa catalogue. Productivity +100%. Each eligible producer must finish a cycle to generate its extras.

## Bonus goods per cycle

The catalogue includes physical island goods assigned to the region, used by its residents or used in local production and construction. Shared goods and imports can appear in several regions. Jack keeps supplying Leather Boots from the earlier Enbesa edition. This module lets the trading post store bonus tractor fuel. Tractor barns still receive fuel carts from an operating Fuel Station. Oil still uses the oil harbour's dedicated storage.

- **Commercial Supply** — Fertiliser.
- **Construction Material** — Mud Bricks, Wanza Timber.
- **Happiness Good** — Clay Pipe, Hibiscus Tea, Leather Boots, Tapestries.
- **Intermediate Good** — Cotton Fabric, Fuel, Glass, Linen, Ornate Candles, Spiced Flour.
- **Resource** — Beeswax, Clay, Cotton, Goose Feathers, Grain, Herbs, Hibiscus Petals, Indigo Dye, Linseed, Lobster, Nice Scrap, Orchid, Paper, Salt, Sanga Cow, Scrap, Special Scrap, Spices, Teff, Tobacco, Wood.
- **Strategic Resource** — Oil.
- **Supply Good** — Canned Food, Ceramics, Dried Meat, Finery, Goat Milk, Hot Sauce, Illuminated Script, Jam, Lanterns, Ponchos, Seafood Stew, Sewing Machines, Soap, Spectacles.

## Availability

The specialist can appear at Emperor Ketema's harbour in Enbesa. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Production - Enbesa` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.

Playing in Enbesa requires Land of Lions.
