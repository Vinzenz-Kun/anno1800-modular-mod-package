# [Gameplay] Residential attractiveness

I wanted homes themselves to count toward the appeal of the cities I built. Enabling this module makes a dense residential district a substantial source of attractiveness, so the layout of your housing carries more weight.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Each residence adds five attractiveness to its island, including shelters, quarters, hotels, skyscrapers and the Skyline Tower.

Ordinary homes contribute to the Culture category. Buildings that already provide attractiveness retain their category and receive five additional points.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Residential Appeal` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
