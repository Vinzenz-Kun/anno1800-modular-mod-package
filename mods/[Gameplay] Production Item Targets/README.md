# [Gameplay] Orchard, chemical and telephone specialists

I made this module for players who like choosing the right person for a job. An orchard, chemical plant or telephone factory can now benefit from specialists you may already own, so it is worth taking another look at your Trade Union inventory.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

More existing Trade Union items can affect orchard recipes, chemical production and the Telephone Manufacturer.

General production equipment reaches orchard and chemical recipes. Electrified-industry equipment reaches chemical recipes. Horticulturalist Hermann can use his productivity skill in orchards, and the Telephone Manufacturer can benefit from carpentry specialists such as the Carpenter. Equip a matching item in a Trade Union that covers the building.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Production Item Targets` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
