# [Acquisition] Specialists at the traders

I wanted to know which harbour to sail to when an island needed a particular specialist. This module sorts six traders’ stock by rarity from the first progression stage. You can use it on its own if you still want to earn each item by trading.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Sir Archibald Blake offers legendary items, Eli Bleakworth epic items and Madame Kahina rare items. Isabel Sarmento, Anne Harlow and Jean La Fortune share common and uncommon stock. The catalogue includes equippable specialists and ordinary equipment and collectibles across the extracted game item catalogue. Active bundle characters join Archibald's stock. The Inuit, Nate and Ketema keep their regional business. Trading rights still apply. Reopen the harbour after a stock refresh or reroll.

Copy this folder into Anno 1800's mods folder. Pair it with `[Acquisition] Research` for both routes. Disable older trader-overhaul or permanent-catalogue mods that also assign these traders' stock.
