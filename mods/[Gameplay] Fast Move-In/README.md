# [Gameplay] Fast residential move-in

I like to see a new district come to life as soon as its services are ready. Enable this module if you want fulfilled needs to translate into residents almost immediately. Pairing it with a needs specialist also takes much of the wait out of workforce growth.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

When a home has space and fulfilled needs, new residents move in at short intervals.

Homes use a 250 ms move-in interval and a 50 ms random interval. The Skyline Tower keeps its already faster 100 ms and 10 ms timings. Skyscrapers, Hacienda quarters, Arctic shelters, scholar residences and tourist hotels are included.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Fast Move-In` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
