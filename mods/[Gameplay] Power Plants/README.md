# [Gameplay] Power plant range and fuel storage

I wanted a working power station to be able to serve distant districts from the same road network. Selecting this module makes plant placement and fuel delivery more forgiving, especially on a large island.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Power plants supply electricity along 2,048 road units. Each fuel-consuming plant holds 500 tons per fuel input.

Supply the power plant with its usual fuel and connect the receiving buildings to its road network. The patch covers oil, gas and fuel power plants, the New World Power Station and the Manola dam. The dam uses its normal production conditions.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Power Plants` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
