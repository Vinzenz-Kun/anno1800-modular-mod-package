# [Gameplay] Instant ship construction

I wanted the option to replace a lost ship as soon as I could afford it. Instant construction makes the shipyard timer almost disappear from fleet planning, so I kept it separate for anyone who enjoys the wait between launches.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Buildable ships and airships complete on the next production tick once the yard has accepted the order.

Sailing and steam shipyards, airship hangars and the extra ships granted by shipbuilding specialists use a one-millisecond construction time. Resource costs, permits, unlocks and the yard queue still apply.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Shipbuilding` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
