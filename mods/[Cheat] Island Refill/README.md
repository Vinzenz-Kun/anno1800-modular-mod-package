# [Cheat] Island Storage Refill

I made the refill a shortcut so you can decide which shortages to solve yourself. One delivery can keep a new settlement running while you lay out its first farms. Using it regularly can supply an entire island and take the place of its trade routes.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Move the camera over an island you own and press **Shift + F9**. The game adds up to **500 t of each available good** to that island's storage, limited by its free space. Press again to supply a larger warehouse. The shortcut works in single-player sessions and can be reassigned in the game's keyboard settings.

Copy `[Cheat] Island Refill` into your Anno 1800 `mods` folder.

Select your own ship with cargo space and press **Shift+F10** to receive the next specialist in the delivery rotation. Disable the older refill mod to avoid duplicate key bindings.
