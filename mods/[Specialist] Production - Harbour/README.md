# [Specialist] Jack of All Seas

I wanted a busy fishing coast to be able to bankroll an entire port. Jack turns production cycles into a source of goods from all four regions, so equipping him is a deliberate choice about how quickly that port grows.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip in a Harbourmaster's Office. Provides electricity to compatible buildings. Every completed production cycle adds one ton of each of the 191 goods across all four regions. Productivity +100%. Each eligible producer must finish a cycle to generate its extras. Covers coastal producers and power-using factories within the office's coverage, including eligible inland factories.

## Bonus goods per cycle

The catalogue includes physical island goods assigned to the region, used by its residents or used in local production and construction. Shared goods and imports can appear in several regions. Jack also supplies materials used in Old Nate's workshop recipes. This module lets the trading post store bonus tractor fuel. Tractor barns still receive fuel carts from an operating Fuel Station. Oil still uses the oil harbour's dedicated storage. Mail and reports use the game's postal storage and delivery rules.
