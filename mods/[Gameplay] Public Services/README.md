# [Gameplay] Public services and palace range

I wanted the freedom to lay out a neighbourhood around one market or public building serving a long stretch of road. This module also gives the Palace and Local Departments wider reach, so demand for duplicate service buildings falls sharply.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Markets, public need providers, the Palace and Local Departments can serve distant neighbourhoods along connected roads.

Public services reach full satisfaction along 2,048 road units and drop to zero at 2,049. The Palace begins with 2,048 street range and gains two units per wing. Local Departments use the Palace's range. Normal staffing, road connections and palace policy conditions apply.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Public Services` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
