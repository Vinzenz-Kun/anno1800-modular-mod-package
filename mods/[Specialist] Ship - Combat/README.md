# [Specialist] Captain Mara Voss, Fleet Gunnery Officer

I wanted one warship to be able to carry a fight when the odds were against it. Assign Mara to the ship you want to strengthen, whether that is a lone escort or the flagship of a larger fleet.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Equip Mara on a warship. Active Healing keeps self-repair running under fire at +500% repair rate. Unwavering raises damage as hull strength falls, up to 1.6 times normal. Adds 1,000 hit points and a local +100% maximum hull bonus. Guns gain +150% base damage, +100% accuracy, +25 attack speed, -40% reload time and +30% range. Damage against sailing ships and buildings rises by 50%. Incoming normal damage falls by 25%, and cannon, torpedo and Big Bertha damage by 80%. The ship gains +30% sight and movement speed and ignores the speed penalty from hull damage.

## Availability

Look for legendary offers from Eli Bleakworth, Anne Harlow or Jean La Fortune. Trading with a pirate requires trade rights. The bundle's optional trader and Research Institute modules add further acquisition routes.

## Installation

Copy the entire `[Specialist] Ship - Combat` folder into Anno 1800's `mods` folder and enable it in the mod browser.
