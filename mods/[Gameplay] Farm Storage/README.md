# [Gameplay] Farm and orchard storage

If you enjoy planning cart routes but dislike seeing a farm stop while it waits for collection, this is the part of the bundle I made for you. A larger buffer gives your carts more time between pickups.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Crop farms, livestock farms, Hacienda farm recipes and orchard recipes can hold four times their usual output before collection.

The multiplier is applied to each production building’s local output buffer. Check the output counter on the farm itself. Cart capacity and island-wide warehouse capacity are separate settings.

## Installation

Copy the `[Gameplay] Farm Storage` folder into your Anno 1800 `mods` directory and enable it in the mod browser. This module works independently. DLC buildings and systems require their respective DLC.
