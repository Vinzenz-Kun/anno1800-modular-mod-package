# Credits

Many of the ideas I wanted for my own islands started with mods that other people took the time to make and share. When I designed Jack of All Lodges for the Arctic, I was following a path that blejky opened with the original Jack of All Trades. When I reworked tourist needs, Sindariel’s changes to venue range and distance penalties gave me a starting point for making a whole island feel like one destination.

I wrote new patches around the way I like to play. Where several ideas helped with the same thing, I brought them together in one module so you could pick up a whole set of related changes at once. The portraits come from Anno 1800 itself. What made this collection possible was the work these modders had already done, often on the exact detail I wanted to change. I’m grateful to every one of them.

If you enjoy one of my versions, please visit the original mod linked below. Try it on its own, leave the creator a note about what you enjoyed, or give it an endorsement. Thank you to everyone whose work gave me a place to begin.
