# [Cheat] Full Influence

I like being able to settle one more island or equip one more Trade Union when I am trying out a city layout. This optional reserve removes most of the pressure to ration influence and leaves you free to run the rest of the economy at its usual difficulty.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Keep **1,000,000 influence available to spend** in single-player. Buying ships or shares, placing buildings, running propaganda and settling islands still commit influence at their usual prices. Those investments count toward the ordinary influence titles and bonuses.

Copy `[Cheat] Permanent Influence` into your Anno 1800 `mods` folder and load your game. Once per simulation second the module checks available influence and tops up any shortfall, up to the capacity reported by the game. The base capacity for human players is set to one million. The automatic top-up runs in single-player.

Anno derives each empire's total influence from its sources and investments. One million is this module's chosen reserve. Earned points and refunds can take you above it. The next purchase can briefly bring your balance below it. Removing the module restores normal base capacity and ends future grants. The game can retain points already credited to the save.
