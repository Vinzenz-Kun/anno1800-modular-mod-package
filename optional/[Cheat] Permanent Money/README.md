# [Cheat] Full Treasury

I made this for saves where I want to build a city without waiting for the next ship, monument or trade agreement to become affordable. A treasury that refills itself takes most of the pressure out of earning a profit, so this is a separate choice from the one-time cash shortcut.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

In single-player, your treasury refills to the **coin limit reported by the game**, including limits above one billion. When the game reports no positive finite limit, the module uses one billion coins as its target.

Once per second of simulation time, the module deposits the missing coins, up to one billion per deposit. Buying a ship or sending a diplomatic gift still charges its normal price. A larger shortfall is paid in installments over successive seconds.

Copy `[Cheat] Permanent Money` into your Anno 1800 `mods` folder and start or load a game. Let the simulation run for a second. Disable the module to stop future top-ups. Coins already received remain in the save.
