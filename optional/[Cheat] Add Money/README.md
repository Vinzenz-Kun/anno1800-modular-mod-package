# [Cheat] Add Money

I wanted a way to pay for one ambitious project and still have a reason to balance the books afterwards. This optional cash grant lets you choose exactly when your empire gets a helping hand.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Press **Shift + F11** in a single-player session to credit **1,000,000 coins** to your empire. The money goes straight to the treasury and can be used in any region. The game enforces its treasury limit. Change the shortcut in the keyboard settings under "Add one million coins."

Copy `[Cheat] Add Money` into your Anno 1800 `mods` folder. It works on its own or alongside the island refill shortcut. For automatic replenishment after every purchase, use the separate `[Cheat] Permanent Money` module.
