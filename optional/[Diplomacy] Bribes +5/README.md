# [Diplomacy] Paid Bribes (+5)

I made this gentler option for games where I still want a difficult neighbour to take time and money to win over. Five points gives each payment a visible effect and makes a larger improvement something you save up for.

This is one module in the Anno 1800 Modular Specialists Bundle. With the full collection enabled, even supplying a city and balancing its budget can become optional. Choose the changes you want for your save.

Open Diplomacy, select a rival or pirate, and offer a **monetary gift**. A successful payment awards **5 reputation points**, up to the normal reputation ceiling. The module sets the gift acceptance chance to 100% at each repetition tier. The recipient's normal fee still applies, including level-based increases. The gift repetition penalty expires after one second. The diplomacy interface also applies its ordinary two-second action cooldown.

The patch covers all eleven rival profiles, including Carl von Malching and Dr. Hugo Mercier, and both pirates, Anne Harlow and Jean La Fortune. The game determines which diplomatic actions your current relationship makes available.

Copy `[Diplomacy] Bribes +5` into your Anno 1800 `mods` folder. Choose one reputation variant. If both are active, the +5 setting takes precedence. Both variants supersede the earlier Rival and Pirate Diplomacy module, which changed flattery.
